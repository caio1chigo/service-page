Warning Signs

Is Your Home Hiding a Water Leak?

Many leaks happen behind walls, under floors, or in crawl spaces. Catching them early can save you from major repairs and high water bills.

  • ◉ Unexplained increase in your water bill
  • ◉ Damp spots, discoloration, or peeling paint on walls or ceilings
  • ◉ Musty odors or signs of mold in certain areas
  • ◉ Sound of running water when all taps are off
  • ◉ Warm spots or wet areas on floors

If you notice any of these signs, it is worth getting a professional inspection before the problem gets worse.

Why It Matters

Small Leaks Can Become Big Problems.

A slow leak might seem minor, but over weeks or months it can cause structural damage, mold growth, and surprisingly high water bills.

  • ✓ Prevent costly structural repairs
  • ✓ Reduce risk of mold and mildew
  • ✓ Lower your monthly water bill
  • ✓ Protect your home's value

Early detection is almost always cheaper and less disruptive than waiting for obvious damage to appear.
